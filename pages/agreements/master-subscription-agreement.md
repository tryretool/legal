## 1. Definitions

“**Affiliate**” means any entity which directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the subject entity. “Control,” for purposes of this definition, means direct or indirect ownership or control of more than 50% of the voting interests of the subject entity.

“**Authorized User**” means an individual who is authorized by Customer to access and use the Retool Platform, including any Custom Apps under Customer’s account, and for whom Customer has purchased a subscription under an Order Form.

“**Confidential Information**” has the meaning given to it in Section 5 (Confidentiality), and (a) for Customer, includes Custom Apps and Customer Data; (b) for Retool, includes non-public information regarding the features, functionality, and performance of the Retool Platform; and (c) for each party, includes the terms and conditions of this Agreement and all Order Forms, as well as business and marketing plans, technology and technical information, product plans and designs, and business processes disclosed by such party.

“**Custom App**” means any application, workflow, or automation that is developed, generated, or modified by or on behalf of Customer with the Retool Platform, including through User Prompts (as defined in Section 2.5 – AI-Powered Building) or manual code editing.

“**Customer Data**” means data, files, and other materials that Customer or an Authorized User uploads, submits, or makes available to the Retool Platform, either directly or indirectly, including through Custom Apps and Non-Retool Services, to be processed on Customer’s behalf.

“**Documentation**” means Retool’s online user guides, and policies, as updated from time to time, accessible via [https://docs.retool.com](https://docs.retool.com) or such successor site.

“**Expert Services**” means ancillary services offered by Retool, if specified under Customer’s Order Form, to facilitate, accelerate or optimize Customer’s use of the Retool Platform under the Agreement.

“**Order Form**” means an ordering document (including any online order) entered into between Retool and Customer, or between Retool and Reseller, that specifies the Retool offerings to be provided under this Agreement.

“**Non-Retool Service**” means any product, service, software, or other materials not provided by Retool that Customer chooses to interoperate with or use in connection with the Retool Platform, such as Customer-Managed Models (as defined in Section 2.5 – AI-Powered Building) and AI assistants and coding tools.

“**Product-Specific Terms**” means the terms, requirements, and policies applicable to specific deployments, products, and features of the Retool Platform, as published at [https://docs.retool.com/legal/pages/agreements/product-specific-terms/](https://docs.retool.com/legal/pages/agreements/product-specific-terms/) and updated from time to time, which form part of this Agreement.

**"Retool Platform"** means Retool’s developer tools and platform, including all components and templates made available by Retool therein, that enable Customer and its Authorized Users to build, run, and manage Custom Apps, as described in the Documentation, and all updates and improvements to the foregoing. “Retool Platform” excludes Non-Retool Services, Expert Services, and Customer Data.

“**Service Term**” means the term for Customer’s access and use of the Retool Platform, as specified in the applicable Order Form.

“**Scope of Use**” means Customer’s entitlements for the Retool Platform as identified in the applicable Order Form.

“**Trial or Beta Product**” means features or functionality of the Retool Platform that Retool may make available to Customer to try, at its option, and which may be designated by Retool as a beta, pilot, limited release, developer preview, non-production, evaluation, or by a similar description.
